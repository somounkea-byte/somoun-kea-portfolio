# ⚡ Quick Start - 5 Minutes to Live Website

## 🎯 Do This Now

### Step 1: Deploy (2 minutes)
1. Go to https://vercel.com/new
2. Drag & drop your WB folder
3. Click "Deploy"
4. Copy your Vercel URL (e.g., `yourproject.vercel.app`)

### Step 2: Update SEO URLs (2 minutes)
Replace `yourvercelurl` in these files with your actual Vercel URL:
- **index.html** - 3 places (search for "yourvercelurl")
- **sitemap.xml** - 8 places (search for "yourvercelurl")
- **robots.txt** - 1 place (search for "yourvercelurl")

### Step 3: Tell Google About Your Site (1 minute)
1. Go to https://search.google.com/search-console
2. Sign in with Google
3. Add URL: Your Vercel URL
4. Click "Request Indexing"

✅ **DONE! Your site is now live and Google knows about it!**
